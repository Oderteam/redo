Order and Chaos in Art

Exploring the dynamic between structure and spontaneity in creative practice.

The book studies order and chaos across disciplines, philosophy, religion, science, psychology, and traces how the same tension appears in art, from antiquity to now.

Written for artists, designers, thinkers and makers, and for anyone curious about how structure and spontaneity work together.
