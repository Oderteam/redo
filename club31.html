Club 31

An ongoing research into order and chaos, told through a returning set of themes: color, geometry, line, asemic writing, material, gesture, text.

color → geometry → line → space → sign → asemic → figure → page → material → gesture → text → new experiment

The ideas behind this research are laid out in the book, Order and Chaos in Art.
