Vision

"Writing without words still carries something. The hand knows what it's doing before the mind catches up."

Tim Oder's practice centers on asemic writing, marks that move like language without settling into it. A gesture held between control and accident, read the way you'd read a face rather than a sentence. The works, the research and the studio all grow from that one idea.

Asemic

"A mark made without knowing exactly where it will land. The mistake isn't corrected out, it's read as information."

Writing that never settles into words, faces and figures built from line, gesture held on paper. Thought, Heads, People: three ways the same practice keeps asking what a mark can carry once it lets go of meaning.
